## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a balance between field-tested agricultural heritage and modern bio-chemical precision. Crafted for agricultural producers, agronomists, and retail distributors, the aesthetic establishes deep institutional trust (drawing from roots established in 1988) while presenting data-dense crop solutions with absolute clarity.

The visual direction merges **Modern Scientific Precision** with **Organic Vitality**:
- **Field Trust & Heritage:** Grounded deep greens anchor stability, safety, and regulatory compliance.
- **Biochemical Innovation:** Vibrant lime and warm harvest gold provide rapid visual cueing for growth stages, nutrient categories, and field application calls to action.
- **Clarity Under Direct Sunlight:** High-contrast text hierarchies, generous touch targets, and tactile surface separation ensure legibility on rugged outdoor mobile devices as well as high-resolution desktop agronomy portals.

## Colors

The color system is rooted in high-contrast natural tones that reflect crop health, soil vitality, and chemical purity.

- **Primary (`#1B5E20` - Forest Emerald):** Represents foundational stability, biological vitality, and enterprise trust. Used for core navigation, high-priority interactive states, and authoritative typography headers.
- **Secondary (`#7CB342` - Growth Lime):** Evokes active photosynthesis and vegetative vigor. Employed for secondary actions, active badges, status indicators, and subtle organic tints.
- **Tertiary (`#F9A825` - Harvest Gold):** Signals yield, ripeness, and decisive action. Reserved strictly for conversion CTAs, urgent action highlights, and critical nutrient alerts.
- **Neutral (`#1F2937` - Deep Charcoal):** Replaces pure black to eliminate harsh optical strain while guaranteeing WCAG AAA compliance on light surfaces.
- **Canvas (`#F8FAF5`):** A custom tinted off-white with an organic green undertone, eliminating glare in direct sunlight while isolating pure white (`#FFFFFF`) card surfaces.
- **Danger Red (`#C82A2B`):** Preserved from institutional safety labels for toxicity warnings, pesticide hazard ratings, and destructive actions.

## Typography

Typography pairs geometric approachability with rigorous structural legibility:

- **Display & Headlines (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its friendly yet structural geometric terminals. Delivers an authoritative, forward-thinking presence across crop category titles, diagnostic dashboards, and marketing hero sections.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Inter`):** Provides clinical clarity for formulation specs, chemical dosage charts, dilution ratios, and field data tables. Tall x-height and distinct apertures maintain maximum legibility on low-resolution field mobile screens.
- **Microcopy & Data Labels:** Always rendered with increased tracking (`0.02em` - `0.05em`) and medium/semibold weights to ensure chemical compositions and batch numbers are instantly distinguishable.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout architecture uses an 8pt baseline rhythm adapted for agricultural responsive interfaces.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column responsive fluid grid with a maximum content container of `1280px`. Outer margins are locked at `2rem` (`margin`), with `1.5rem` (`gutter`) gutters between structural columns.
- **Tablet (640px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` outer margins and `1rem` (`gutter-sm`) gutters.
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (`margin-mobile`) margins. Dense technical cards collapse into single-column vertical stacks.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Component internals use `space-sm` (8px) and `space-md` (16px) for compact efficiency, while section separations scale from `space-lg` (24px) to `space-xl` (40px) to provide breathable visual grouping between complex chemical datasets.

## Elevation & Depth

This system utilizes soft, organic ambient layering over harsh geometric drops, simulating soft natural sunlight:

- **Base Canvas:** Flat `#F8FAF5` ground layer.
- **Surface Level 1 (Cards, Product Tiles):** Solid `#FFFFFF` fill paired with a subtle structural perimeter stroke (`1px solid #E2E8DC`) and an ambient tinted shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(27, 94, 32, 0.05)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Hover States, Floating Quick-Filters, Popovers):** Elevated with `0 10px 25px -4px rgba(27, 94, 32, 0.10), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Modals, Slide-over Agronomy Drawers):** `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.18)` against a backdrop blur overlay (`rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.40)` with `blur(6px)`).
- **Tactile Hierarchy:** Interactive elements avoid heavy skeletal borders in favor of distinct surface color shifts and low-saturation foliage-tinted depth.

## Shapes

The shape system employs consistent `16px` (`rounded-lg` / `1rem`) corner radii for foundational components, imparting a modern, accessible, and organic presence.

- **Cards & Data Panels:** Exactly `16px` (`1rem`) border radius, creating clean containment for product specifications, dosage instructions, and test plots.
- **Input Fields & Form Elements:** Standardized to `8px` (`0.5rem`) radius to retain clear structural affordance and form discipline.
- **Action Buttons & Badges:** Use either `8px` (`0.5rem`) for standard operational UI or full pill radius (`9999px`) for category filter chips, product tags, and status badges to evoke organic seed and leaf contours.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Brand Green):** Solid `#1B5E20` background, pure white `#FFFFFF` text (`label-lg`), `8px` border radius, padding `12px 24px`. Hover: `#144617`. Focus ring: `3px solid rgba(124, 179, 66, 0.4)`.
- **CTA / Conversion (Harvest Gold):** Solid `#F9A825` background, `#1F2937` deep charcoal text for high contrast. Used exclusively for "Buy Now", "Inquire Distributor", or "Download Dosage Sheet". Hover: `#F57F17`.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background, `1.5px` border in `#1B5E20`, `#1B5E20` text. Hover: `#1B5E20` background at `6%` opacity.

### Badges & Category Chips
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height `28px`, padding `0 12px`.
- **Bio-fertilizer / Growth:** `#7CB342` at `15%` background tint, `#1B5E20` text weight `600`.
- **Chemical Formulation / Hazard Warnings:** Soft `#C82A2B` at `10%` background tint, `#C82A2B` text with an accompanying SVG symbol.
- **Filter Chips:** Interactive, toggleable pills with `#E2E8DC` border in unselected state; fill shifts to `#1B5E20` with white text upon activation.

### Cards
- **Product & Formulation Card:** Pure `#FFFFFF` background, `16px` radius, `1px solid #E2E8DC` border.
- **Card Padding:** `20px` internal padding.
- **Header:** Accommodates high-contrast product branding, followed by technical composition in `body-sm` (`#555555`), dynamic badge arrays, and persistent primary action footer.

### Form Inputs & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height `48px` (touch-optimized for outdoor use), `#FFFFFF` background, `1.5px solid #E2E8DC` border, `8px` radius. Active focus: `#1B5E20` border with soft lime glow (`rgba(124, 179, 66, 0.25)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `20px × 20px` minimum bounding box. Checked state features `#1B5E20` fill with white checkmark glyph.

### Agronomy Metric & Dosage Tables
- Zebra-striped data rows using alternating `#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAF5`.
- Header row styled in `#1B5E20` with crisp white text or subtle neutral grey (`#E2E8DC`) borders, ensuring clarity for dosage rates (e.g., ml/acre, gm/liter).
